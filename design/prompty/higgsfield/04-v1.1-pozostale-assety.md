# v1.1: pozostałe assety

Zaczynamy dopiero po starcie v1 i pierwszej rozmowie (koncepcja §10). Wszystkie te assety wyprowadzamy ze zwycięskiego
hero, który jest referencją stylu.

Dwie zasady dla każdego promptu z referencjami:
- Na początku stoi **zdanie relacji**: „Image 1 is … use it only for …; do not copy its layout”.
- Na końcu doklejamy blok **STYLE** z `01-hero-runda-1.md`.

---

## A0: karta stylu (referencja dla wszystkich dalszych generacji)
**Ustawienia:** Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 4K, 2 warianty. Image 1 to zwycięski still hero.
```
Image 1 is the approved hero illustration. Use it only as the reference for drawing style, outline weight, colors, shading and the module housing design; do not copy its layout.
Horizontal 16:9 flat-lay of separate parts on a plain background: eighteen objects in a tidy grid of six columns and three rows, all at the same scale, evenly spaced with generous empty space, none touching or overlapping.
Row one: a straight cable segment, a 90-degree cable bend, a 45-degree cable bend, a cable tie, a large plug, a matching round wall socket with a small LED.
Row two: a cable sleeve connector, a rounded junction box, one glowing amber LED, one small glowing sea-green packet of light, a lavender neural node with branching dendrite fibers, one module housing with a blank paper panel.
Row three: six module housings whose paper panels show only simple pictograms: a shelf with three plain boxes; a blank sheet with grey bars sliding into a slot; a speech bubble with three dots; a stack of sheets with grey bars; an empty grid of cells; a gear.
```
**Odrzuty.** Odrzucamy generację, jeśli ma:
1. tekst, podpisy albo próbki kolorów;
2. obiekty, które się stykają;
3. liczbę obiektów inną niż 18;
4. różne grubości obrysu;
5. perspektywę;
6. tło, które nie jest płaskie;
7. obudowę inną niż w hero;
8. połysk albo efekt 3D.

## A1d: hero na desktop (tylko jeśli Web Analytics pokaże ponad 30% wejść z desktopu)
**Ustawienia:** Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 4K, 2–4 warianty. Image 1 to zwycięski A1m, Image 2 to A0. Wynik zmniejszamy do 1920×1080.
```
Image 1 is the approved vertical illustration and Image 2 is its parts sheet. Use them only for drawing style, colors, outline weight and the exact module designs; build a new horizontal composition, not a crop or a stretch of Image 1.
Horizontal 16:9 illustration of the same enormous machine of wiring, filling the whole frame from edge to edge, in three depth layers.
Left 45% of the frame: the machine continues but sinks into deep shadow: only dim lavender fibers and graphite cables drawn barely lighter than the background, an even, low-contrast tangle with no glowing lights and no paper panels.
Right 55%: full-contrast machinery: the lavender neural web behind; at least forty thick graphite cables and ten rounded junction boxes in the middle; the same six module housings with the same pictograms in front. The glowing lavender neural knot sits at about 72% width and 45% height, with small sea-green packets of light in the cables that lead into it.
From the knot, ONE thick main cable of constant width carries a single amber glow toward the lower left with 45- and 90-degree bends; its last 15% runs perfectly straight and vertical and leaves the bottom edge at 40% of the frame width from the left. The bottom edge stays clear of other objects near it.
```
**Odrzuty:**
- wszystkie odrzuty A1m;
- lewe 45% powyżej 75% wysokości jest jaśniejsze niż `#2A313A`;
- pień wychodzi poza 37–43% szerokości;
- kadr wygląda na przycięty albo rozciągnięty A1m.

**Ruch:** prompt z `02-ruch-hero.md` z dwiema podmianami: `along the bright cables on the right` oraz `The dark left side stays dark and still.`

## A2: przepływ zamówienia (sekcja 01, okno 16:9)
**Domyślnie:** still (AVIF) i światło w CSS nad nim, czyli pakiety na zmierzonej wysokości kabla i diody w takcie 2 s.
Wideo z Higgsfield to opcja: strategia V-B i blokada statyki.
**Ustawienia:** Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 2K, referencje: hero i A0.
```
Horizontal 16:9. Four module housings in a straight row at exactly mid-height, evenly spaced, chained left to right by one thick graphite cable of constant width that enters from the left edge at exactly mid-height and leaves the fourth module toward the right edge. Their paper panels show simple pictograms: (1) a speech bubble with three dots, (2) a shelf with three plain boxes, (3) a blank sheet with grey bars sliding into a slot, (4) a tray holding one blank sheet with grey bars. From module 2 a thinner cable drops straight down to a small fifth module with a round bell pictogram. Generous empty background above and below; nothing touches the frame edges except the main cable.
```
**Ruch (opcjonalnie):**
```
Static tripod shot, locked-off camera. The illustration holds perfectly still exactly as in the first frame. Only light travels: a steady stream of small sea-green packets glides from left to right inside the main cable, through each module in turn, and each module's LED glows amber as a packet passes. Now and then one amber packet drops down the side cable and the bell module's LED glows. 24 fps.
```

## A4: ikony usług (Recraft V4.1, tryb vector)
**Paleta:** w Higgsfield ustawiamy ją przez Soul HEX z pliku PNG z 5 polami koloru: `#05070A`, `#1F262E`, `#F2EDE3`, `#FFB938`, `#39D0C0`.
Lawendę `#9B8CFF` dodajemy tylko w ikonie 4.
```
Set of six flat vector icons in one row, equal size, generous spacing, plain background. Every icon is the same rounded module box: dark graphite body, warm paper front panel, one round port on its left side, one small amber dot in the top-right corner, a thin pale highlight along the top edge, and a bold near-black outline of one equal width. Flat fills, no gradients, no shadows. Front-panel pictograms: 1) a shelf with three plain boxes, 2) a blank sheet with grey bars sliding into a slot, 3) two plugs joined by an adapter, 4) a speech bubble with three dots and a small lavender node, 5) a tray holding one blank sheet, 6) three plain bars of different heights. Simple bold shapes readable at 40 pixels. No text, no letters, no numbers.
```
**Po eksporcie:**
1. Dzielimy plik na 6 osobnych ikon.
2. Usuwamy tło.
3. Wyrównujemy grubość obrysu.
4. Diodzie dopisujemy `class="led"`.
5. Przepuszczamy przez SVGO. Każda ikona waży najwyżej 8 KB.

W v1 ikony rysuje Claude Design jako proste SVG w obudowie modułu. A4 może je później zastąpić.
